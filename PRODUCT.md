# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Two audiences, to be confirmed in priority order: recruiters and companies evaluating a backend/full-stack hire, and people or teams who may later be pointed to Lattice Systems for services. Both arrive wanting to know quickly who Daniel is, what he has built, and how to reach him.

## Product Purpose

Personal site of Daniel Ojeda, Backend & Full-Stack developer, on the `.me` domain. It builds his personal brand as a developer, shows real work and experience, and sends interested visitors to contact him or to Lattice Systems. Success: a visitor understands his profile and gets in touch.

## Positioning

Developer-first personal brand with real professional experience and projects in git. Daniel is the founder of Lattice Systems, a consultancy and services venture that is just starting; this site is the human face behind it.

## Operating Context

Two separate sites: this one (personal, `.me` domain) and Lattice Systems (`latticesystems.dev`, separate project). They link to each other but do not share a page. Content distribution will also run through LinkedIn and an Instagram account Daniel will resume.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Next.js App Router, shadcn/ui on Base UI, Tailwind v4 (existing scaffold).
- Bilingual ES/EN from the start.
- Currently a placeholder contact form; no real pages yet.
- Provisional decisions (confirmed 2026-10-24, revisable once Lattice Systems has clients):
  - Audience priority: recruiters and companies first; services are a short section pointing to `latticesystems.dev`.
  - Primary CTA: "Contáctame" / "Contact me"; secondary: "Ver proyectos" / "View projects". One CTA per screen. CV download lives in the footer or experience section.
  - Logo: none for now; a typographic wordmark using the handle "dy.ojeda", where the dot is part of the identity. A monogram may come later.
  - Structure: single page with anchors, ES/EN: hero, selected projects (3-4), experience, stack, Lattice Systems block, contact. Per-project detail pages are added once content exists.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Daniel Ojeda (personal brand); handle/wordmark: dy.ojeda (the dot is intentional). Lattice Systems is a separate brand with its own site.
- Languages: Spanish and English.
- Visual stance (confirmed 2026-10-24 after four exploration rounds): the category standard for developer portfolios, executed at full craft, not a conceptual metaphor. Reference bar: gauravgrv.vercel.app (favorite) and ezradesmonds.my.id. Must support light and dark mode, purposeful animation, and read clearly as a developer.
- Optional personal signature: a Sinaloa-style western hat as a single line icon in the UI's own stroke (about section, favicon or 404). Never a theme, never caricature, never narco-associated imagery. Leaving it out is acceptable.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real work experience at companies and projects in git repositories (to be selected and described by Daniel).
- LinkedIn content and an Instagram account are planned, not yet active.
- No clients, testimonials, case studies or metrics for Lattice Systems exist yet. Do not fabricate any.

## Product Principles

- Real over inflated: show only verifiable work, experience and projects.
- Daniel is the proof; Lattice Systems is the pointer, never a pretend track record.
- Bilingual is structural, not an afterthought.
- Fast path to contact from any page.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific requirement established; default to WCAG AA.
